# Virality Director V1

The Virality Director is the **production gate** that sits before NerveStudio.

It exists to stop us from spending time or compute on the first idea that sounds good.

## Research rules implemented

### 1. Idea before production
Generate many concepts. Score them before scripting/rendering.

### 2. Core / Casual / New
A breakout concept should work for:
- the core niche audience,
- people casually interested in the topic,
- people who do not already know the creator/brand.

### 3. Hook = Promise + Proof
A hook should tell the viewer what they get and give them a reason to believe the claim.

### 4. Packaging before filming
Title and thumbnail strength are scored before production.

### 5. Stakes
The viewer needs a concrete reason to care.

### 6. Funnel fit
Do not judge every video only by views. A top-of-funnel discovery video and a bottom-of-funnel conversion video have different jobs.

### 7. Shareability
Prefer concepts people want to send to another person.

## Gate

- **85-100: PRODUCE**
- **70-84.99: ITERATE**
- **below 70: KILL**
- hard failure on audience/hook can kill an idea regardless of total
- weak packaging returns **REPACKAGE**

These are internal workflow thresholds, not claims about YouTube's recommendation algorithm.

## Planned workflow

```
research/trend pool
    ↓
100 candidate ideas
    ↓
Virality Director score
    ↓
top 10
    ↓
10 title variants each
    ↓
3+ thumbnail concepts each
    ↓
package re-score
    ↓
top 1-3 productions
    ↓
Clipper / NerveStudio
    ↓
publish
    ↓
analytics feedback
```

## V2 feedback loop

Use owner-authorized YouTube data and our internal edit lineage to learn:
- opening hook performance,
- retention curve,
- title/thumbnail packaging changes,
- topic/language/format cohorts,
- shares and subscriber conversion,
- production cost and contribution margin.

Do not blend public competitor counters with private owner analytics as if they were the same evidence class.
